# Research brief: Koach Moach (Brain Power) family games

Product: a monthly employee-wellbeing program for organizations. Short
body-and-brain games (1-3 minutes each, about 10-minute sessions) that
working parents play at home with their children aged 5-12, wrapped in a
monthly company-wide challenge with points, a team leaderboard and an
aggregate report for HR.

Buyer: HR and employee-wellbeing managers in Israeli companies with a few
hundred to a few thousand employees.
User: employee parents and their children aged 5-12.

Core problem: movement and family quality time disappear from working
parents' routine, and company wellbeing programs do not reach employees'
homes and do not last.

Secondary benefits to investigate: children's attention, self-regulation
and emotional regulation, parents' energy and mental performance.

- Prefer short tables over long prose.
